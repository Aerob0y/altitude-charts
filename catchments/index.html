Who lives closer to which airport?

Pick an airport and its competitors. Each area is coloured by population density or by how many minutes of driving the airport saves over the nearest competitor.

Beta. Built from saved drive-time snapshots. Coverage is limited and figures may change as the source data is checked.

Areas within the radius (straight line) of the airport are shown. The gold outline surrounds the combined areas where the airport is at least the chosen number of minutes closer by road than every selected competitor.

The outline uses a 150 m display margin to close small gaps in the saved boundaries. Totals use the original areas.
