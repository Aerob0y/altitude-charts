AIRPORT CATCHMENT MAPS Beta

Where each airport is the closest by road

One map for each of 43 airports in New Zealand, Australia and Ireland. Each area is coloured by how many minutes closer it is to the airport by road than to the nearest other major airport. Where no major airport is within two hours, the map shows drive time instead.

Teal areas are closer to the airport; ochre areas are closer to a rival. Open the live map to change rivals and settings, or download the image and the data behind it.

Each airport also has maps with a 30- and 60-minute cut-off: the areas where it is at least that much closer than the nearest major airport.

Population: Stats NZ (New Zealand), the ABS (Australia) and the CSO Census 2022 (Ireland, Electoral Divisions, with boundaries from Tailte Éireann). Drive times calculated with OpenRouteService. Where a road time is missing, a straight-line estimate at 60 km/h is used. Each card counts everyone living in areas at least as close to the airport by road as to any other major airport, the same total the live map shows at 0 minutes. Northern Ireland is not included, so Irish maps near the border overstate the Republic's airports.
