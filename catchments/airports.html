AIRPORT CATCHMENT MAPS Beta

Where each airport is the closest by road

One map for each of 43 airports in New Zealand, Australia and Ireland. Each area is coloured by how many minutes closer it is to the airport by road than to the nearest other major airport. Where no major airport is within two hours, the map shows drive time instead.

Teal areas are closer to the airport; ochre areas are closer to a rival. Open the live map to change rivals and settings, or download the image and the data behind it.

Population: Stats NZ (New Zealand), the ABS (Australia) and the CSO Census 2022 (Ireland, Electoral Divisions, with boundaries from Tailte Éireann). Drive times calculated with OpenRouteService. Where a road time is missing, a straight-line estimate at 60 km/h is used. "People closer by 30+ minutes" counts everyone living in areas at least 30 minutes closer to the airport by road than to any other major airport; the live map starts at 0 minutes, so its catchment total is larger until you raise "Minutes saved to count". Northern Ireland is not included, so Irish maps near the border overstate the Republic's airports.
