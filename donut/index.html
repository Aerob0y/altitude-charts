How to read it

Areas are Stats NZ statistical areas (SA2s), roughly suburbs. Distances are straight-line from Britomart. The grey and teal bars use separate scales, shown in the key, so compare grey with grey and teal with teal.

New homes are dwellings consented, not completed. Stats NZ estimates most consented homes are built, about nine months later. A consent counts one home whatever its size. Population growth from the Census shows where people actually went. The latest year is part of a year.

Population is the 2023 Census usually resident count. For growth, the grey bar is the 2013 Census count. Areas that lost people show an orange bar.

Source: Stats NZ building consents and Census usually resident population counts.
