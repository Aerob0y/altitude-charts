Dunedin Airport: a transparent aeronautical pricing model

Client
Dunedin Airport
Type
Consulting
Service
Pricing and financial modelling

I built a robust and transparent aeronautical pricing model aligned with Commerce Commission guidelines. The model kept charges fair for airlines while supporting the airport's financial sustainability.

It was designed to be used: clearer inputs, improved usability, and scenario analysis so the airport can test options for future planning.

Consulting · Capacity and scheduling

Milford Sound Port: a fair scheduling system for a busy port

Client
Milford Sound Port
Type
Consulting
Service
Capacity and scheduling

I developed an efficient and fair scheduling system for Milford Sound Port, to manage increasing demand while keeping operations safe and effective.

Through data analysis and stakeholder consultation, I created a structured scheduling framework: defined parameters, a baseline schedule and scheduling rules.

The final system makes better use of resources, aligns boat movements with port capacity, and reduces delays and operational risk.
