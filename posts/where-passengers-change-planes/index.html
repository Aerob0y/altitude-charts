Aviation · 12 Nov 2026

Where passengers change planes.

For a lot of people, their flight is only one leg of the trip. This map shows where they change planes.

Each line is a route to, from or within the US and Canada, sized by seats. Each airport is coloured by the share of its passengers who are connecting, rather than starting or ending their trip there.

Four airports stand out: Atlanta (85% connecting), Salt Lake City (77%), Minneapolis (73%) and Detroit (66%). All four are Delta hubs.

The big gateway cities are the opposite. Only 11% of passengers at New York JFK are connecting, 20% at Los Angeles, and almost none at Orlando or Las Vegas. People fly there because that's where they're going.
