Where visitors sleep

Where New Zealanders live, next to where visitors spend their nights.

Most people live in a handful of cities. Visitors spread much further. Fiordland, Mackenzie, Kaikōura and Queenstown each host more than 120 guest nights a year for every person who lives there. Auckland and Waikato host about 5 and 3.

How I made it: each region's guest nights are shared across its small areas in line with where people live. That keeps the totals right, but within a region it puts visitors where residents are, not exactly where the hotels are.

Source: MBIE Accommodation Data Programme, guest nights in commercial accommodation (hotels, motels, holiday parks, backpackers and lodges; not holiday homes or Airbnb), all property types, year to September 2026; Stats NZ estimated resident population by SA2 at 30 June 2025; Stats NZ regional tourism organisation areas 2020.
Units: people per km² and guest nights per km² a year. About 2% of New Zealand's guest nights are not reported by any region; they are shared across Rangitikei, Tararua, Horowhenua, Waimate and Kawerau, which MBIE does not report separately.
