Economy · 20 Nov 2026

Businesses are upbeat. Households are not.

How does New Zealand's mood compare with other countries?

The OECD turns business and consumer surveys into an index where 100 is each country's long-run average. Above 100 is more confident than usual.

New Zealand business confidence reached 102.8 in September 2026, the highest since February 2014. It is also the highest of the five countries I track. In September the UK, the US and Ireland were between 97.7 and 100.8, and Australia was 99.9 in its latest month, August.

Households feel differently. New Zealand consumer confidence was 97.9 in September, and it has been below its long-run average every month since February 2020.

I wonder which of the two is reading the economy right.
