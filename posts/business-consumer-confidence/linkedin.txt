Businesses are upbeat. Households are not.

In the September 2026 data, the OECD business confidence index for New Zealand reached 102.8, its highest since February 2014.

The OECD puts each index on a scale where 100 is its own long-run average. These are survey measures of confidence, rather than growth forecasts. That compares each country with its own history. It does not mean New Zealand has the strongest economy.

Interestingly, consumer confidence was 97.9, still below its long-run average. New Zealand also had the highest business reading among the five countries tracked. Will this business optimism turn into more hiring and investment? When will households start to feel it?

Source: OECD business and consumer confidence indicators. Monthly index, long-run average = 100.
