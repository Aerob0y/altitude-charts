Is pay keeping up with prices?

Pay rises aren’t as good as they look.

Over the last 10 years, average hourly earnings in New Zealand rose about 4.2% a year. Take out inflation and it’s closer to 0.9%.

The best-looking pay rises were some of the worst for buying power. In the year to March 2023, pay was up 7.6%, the fastest in a decade. But prices were rising almost as fast, so pay was only 0.9 points ahead. A year earlier, pay was 2 points behind prices.

Right now pay is up 2.9% and prices 3.7%. That’s the second quarter in a row of going backwards.

Over 20 years, pay after inflation is up about 22%. GDP per working-age person, after inflation, is up about 14%. So I don’t think pay has fallen behind the economy. It looks more like the economy per person just hasn’t grown much.

It’s an average across the country, so your own pay and bills may tell a different story.
