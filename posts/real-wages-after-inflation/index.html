Is pay keeping up with prices?

Wages are still rising. The question is whether they are rising faster than prices.

The chart shows annual wage growth after taking off annual inflation. Above zero, pay is buying more than a year earlier. Below zero, it is buying less.

In the June 2026 quarter, hourly earnings were up 2.9% on a year earlier, but consumer prices were up 4.1%. That is a fall of 1.2 percentage points in what pay can buy. Private sector wages, public sector wages and the labour cost index all fell behind as well.

It ends a good run. Hourly earnings beat inflation for 15 quarters in a row, from September 2022 to March 2026. Before that, they fell behind for four quarters, from September 2021 to June 2022.

I wonder how long it takes for pay to catch up this time.
