A pay rise can still leave you buying less.

In the year to September, average hourly earnings in New Zealand rose 2.9%. Consumer prices rose 3.7%.

Put another way: $100 of hourly pay a year ago is now about $102.90, but a basket of goods that cost $100 now costs about $103.70.

That's the second quarter in a row that prices have grown faster than pay, after 15 quarters where pay was ahead. The gap has narrowed a little since June, and public sector pay is now close to level. Private sector pay is still well behind.

It's an average across the country, so it won't describe everyone's pay rise, and your own costs will differ from the CPI basket.

I'll be watching whether the next figures get back above zero.
