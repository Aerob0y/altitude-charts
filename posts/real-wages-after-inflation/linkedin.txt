Pay rises aren't as good as they look.

Over the last 20 years, average hourly earnings in New Zealand have doubled. Take out inflation and they're up about 21%.

That's still a bit ahead of the economy. GDP per working-age person, after inflation, is up about 14% over the same time. So I don't think pay has fallen behind the economy. It looks more like the economy per person just hasn't grown much.

Right now pay is up 2.9% on a year ago and prices 3.7%. That's the second quarter in a row of pay going backwards.

Source: Stats NZ via RBNZ. Data to September 2026 (GDP to June 2026).
