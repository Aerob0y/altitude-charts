Aviation · 12 Nov 2026

Two ways to fly.

The US and Canada and Europe each put about 1.3 to 1.5 billion seats a year in the air. But their networks look nothing alike.

In the US and Canada, almost everyone flies within their own country. In Europe, most people cross a border.

US and Canada: 84% domestic

84% of seats stay within the US or within Canada. Only 3% cross the US–Canada border.

Europe: 60% cross-border

60% of seats fly between European countries. Only 24% stay within one country, mostly Russia, Turkey, Spain and Italy.

The big hubs

Atlanta is the busiest airport on either map, with 63 million departing seats a year. Europe's busiest are London Heathrow and Istanbul, at 51 million each.

And the busiest route on both maps is the same one: London Heathrow to New York JFK, with 1.8 million seats each way.
