A migration gain can hide a loss of New Zealanders

New Zealand gained 22,600 people through migration in the year to September 2026. But I think that single number hides two quite different stories.

There was a net gain of 60,400 non-New Zealand citizens, alongside a net loss of 37,800 New Zealand citizens. Net means arrivals minus departures, so people are moving in both directions.

On these figures, New Zealand has lost more of its own citizens than it gained in every 12-month period since 2001, apart from the Covid years. A growing population can sit alongside a steady loss of New Zealanders.

Citizenship is the passport someone used, not where they came from or went. And these are provisional estimates, so they'll be revised. I'd watch both sides of the flow rather than the total.

Source: Stats NZ, International migration: September 2026. Year ended September 2026; provisional estimates.
