A migration gain can hide a loss of New Zealanders

New Zealand gained 22,600 people through migration in the year to September 2026. Behind that, about 136,100 people arrived to live here and 113,500 left.

Split by passport, it's two different stories: a net gain of 60,400 non-New Zealand citizens and a net loss of 37,800 New Zealand citizens.

That loss isn't new. In every 12-month period of the last 20 years, apart from Covid, more New Zealanders left than came home. It peaked at about 45,900 in the year to April 2024. I'd watch both sides of the flow, not just the total.

Source: Stats NZ. Provisional estimates, rounded.
