How full is full? It depends where you stay.

New Zealand accommodation was 48.6% occupied in September. Queenstown was at 75.7%.

That gap is why I prefer looking at regions before drawing conclusions from the national average.

In September 2026, occupancy was 65.4% in Auckland and 55.3% in Christchurch, compared with 22.9% in Northland. These figures cover commercial accommodation with at least six stay units, across property types.

Occupancy measures the share of available accommodation units that were used. Guest nights count people: two people staying three nights make six guest nights. The measures answer different questions.

I would use guest nights to understand demand, then occupancy to see how that demand sits against the rooms, beds and sites on offer. Neither tells me what an operator earned.

September is also just one month. Summer destinations and ski destinations have different busy seasons. Before calling a region quiet or stretched, I would compare the same month in earlier years and look at its mix of accommodation.
