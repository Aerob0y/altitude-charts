We're so (not) back.

Travel volumes are approaching 2019 levels, but it's not hitting quite the same.

In the 12 months to August 2026, about 3.08 million NZ resident arrivals were recorded, 0.6% below 2019. So we're back to travel. But 3.71 million overseas visitors arrived in New Zealand, 5% below 2019. So the tourists aren't.

The Australians are back but long-haul is on struggle street, with Asia and Europe below 2019 levels. This seems to be explained in part by the stunning success of Queenstown, which is well above 2019 levels. Outside of Queenstown, Australian visitor numbers are 5% below 2019.

In part there seems to be a reorientation on why we travel. Business, education and conference travel is all down. Holidays and visiting friends and relatives are neutral.

Source: Stats NZ international travel, unpublished monthly passenger data.
