Who's back since Covid? Holidaymakers, mostly.

In the 12 months to August 2026, about 3.71 million overseas visitors arrived in New Zealand, against 3.89 million in 2019. About 5% short overall, but the mix has changed.

Australians are up about 4% on 2019. The Americas are level. Europe is down about 16%, and China and Hong Kong about 20%.

Holiday visitors and people visiting friends and family are back at 2019 levels. Business and education visitors are both down about 40%.

Kiwis are travelling as much as before, just not to the same places. Trips to Japan are up about 54% on 2019, and to Vietnam about 45%. Trips to the United States are down about 36%.

Source: Stats NZ international travel, unpublished monthly passenger data.
