We're so (not) back.

In the 12 months to August 2026, about 3.08 million New Zealand residents arrived home from overseas, 0.6% below 2019. So we're back to travelling. But 3.71 million overseas visitors arrived, 4.5% below 2019. So the tourists aren't.

Australians are back. China and Hong Kong are down about 20% and Europe about 16%. Queenstown is up 58% on 2019, and outside Queenstown Australian visitors are about 5% down.

Business, education and conference travel are all down. Holidays and visiting friends and relatives are neutral.

Full article, with all the charts: https://csv.co.nz/posts/who-is-visiting/

Source: Stats NZ international travel, unpublished monthly passenger data.
