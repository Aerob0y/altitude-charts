September beat 2019. Who is visiting?

New Zealand welcomed more overseas visitors this September than in any previous September.

Stats NZ counted 273,000 overseas visitor arrivals in September 2026, compared with 261,800 in September 2019. That puts the month about 4% above its pre-Covid level.

Australia supplied 53% of arrivals, up from 51% in September 2019. China supplied 12%, up from 11%. The United States and United Kingdom had the same rounded shares as before: 6% and 3%.

I find the mix as useful as the total. An airport, hotel or tour operator needs to know which markets it serves. A national record can still feel very different from one business to the next.

These are trips, so someone visiting twice is counted twice. And one strong September does not tell us how the whole summer will go. I will be watching whether the stronger arrivals continue through the peak season.

Source: Stats NZ, International travel: September 2026. Released 12 November 2026.
