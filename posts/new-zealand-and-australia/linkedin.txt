Australia looks to be pulling ahead of New Zealand

I put the two side by side on five measures.

After inflation, wages in both countries are below 2019: New Zealand about 7% below, Australia about 5%. Wage growth has slowed to 2.1% here, while Australia is still at 3.5%.

Unemployment is 5.7% in New Zealand and 4.6% in Australia, having been level in late 2022. New Zealand still leads on participation. Household spending after inflation is down 1.8% here and up 2.7% there.

I'll be watching whether our wage growth picks up.

Source: Stats NZ via RBNZ, ABS, OECD.
