Is ageing why government spending keeps rising?

I'd always assumed it was. So I checked, from 2000/01 to 2025/26.

Core Crown spending went from 29.9% to 32.0% of GDP. NZ Super (4.3% to 5.4%) and health (5.4% to 7.2%) together rose 2.9 points, more than the whole increase.

The number of people on Super has more than doubled, to about 959,000. About three-quarters of the extra real Super bill is simply more people. The rest is bigger payments.

There were 5.5 people aged 15 to 64 for every person aged 65+ in 2000/01. Now it's 3.7, heading for 2.9 by 2048.

I couldn't find health spending by age, so I can't say how much of the health rise is ageing. What would you want to see next?

Source: Treasury and Stats NZ. Percent of GDP and 2026 NZ dollars, June years.
