Are we spending less?

The same number on the till can hide a very different story.

Card spending in New Zealand was NZ$9,683 million in September 2026, seasonally adjusted. Three years earlier it was NZ$9,712 million. In dollars, almost nothing had changed.

Prices had changed, though. Using the consumers price index, that September 2023 spending was worth NZ$10,603 million in September 2026 prices. On this measure, spending fell about 9% over three years.

Even compared with September 2025, spending rose 1.1% in dollars but fell 2.5% after inflation.

I find the inflation-adjusted line more useful when asking how much spending has recovered. A business can have steady sales in dollars while its customers have less purchasing power.

This covers card payments, rather than all household spending, and uses the broad CPI to adjust prices. It also measures the total, rather than spending per person.

I will be watching for growth that gets ahead of prices.

Explore the chart: https://csv.co.nz/ect/?view=nzd-millions&real=1

Source: Stats NZ electronic card transactions and CPI via RBNZ. Monthly, seasonally adjusted; September 2026 prices.
