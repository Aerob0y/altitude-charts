Are we spending less?

In dollars, New Zealand card spending looks flat. It was NZ$9,683 million in September 2026 and NZ$9,712 million three years earlier.

After inflation, it is a different picture. In September 2026 prices, spending is down about 9% in three years. In the ten years to 2019 it grew about 4% a year after inflation, so this looks like a break from the trend to me.

Fuel is down most, about 32% since 2019 after inflation. Durables are down about 14% and hospitality about 10%. Everyday consumables are up about 3%.

This covers card payments only, not all household spending, and I have used the broad consumers price index. I'd be cautious about reading too much into one measure.

Full post: https://csv.co.nz/posts/real-card-spending/

Source: Stats NZ electronic card transactions and CPI via RBNZ. Monthly, seasonally adjusted; September 2026 prices.
