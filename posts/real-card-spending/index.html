Are we spending less?

Card spending in New Zealand has barely moved in 3 years. Accounting for inflation, that's a 9% drop from Sep 2023 to Sep 2026. Even worse, Sep 2023 is barely different from Sep 2019. A 9% drop over 7 years. Wild. Some of that is fuel, where consumption is dropping over time, but even after accounting for that, every category looks weak.
