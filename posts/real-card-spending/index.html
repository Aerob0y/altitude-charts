Are we spending less?

In dollars, card spending has gone nowhere for three years. Take out inflation and it has fallen.

New Zealanders spent $9,683 million on cards in September 2026, seasonally adjusted. In September 2023 it was $9,712 million, almost exactly the same.

Prices rose over those three years, though. In today's prices, September 2023 spending was worth $10,603 million. So in real terms, card spending is down about 9%. Even against a year ago, it is up 1.1% in dollars but down 2.5% after inflation.

Spending is flat on the till, but people are getting less for it.
