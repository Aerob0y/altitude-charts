Jet fuel just hit a record in New Zealand dollars

A barrel of jet fuel cost a record NZ$348 on 30 September. That is more than at the 2022 spike.

I charted the price of jet fuel back to 2017. It jumped in March this year, from about US$95 a barrel in February to US$155. In New Zealand dollars it reached NZ$348 on 30 September, above the April 2022 high of NZ$326.

In US dollars it is still below the 2022 peak of US$213. The difference is our weaker dollar. In 2017 one NZ dollar bought about 71 US cents; this year it buys about 58. That is why the two lines drift apart as you go back in time.

Fuel is priced in US dollars, so New Zealand airlines pay more when our dollar falls. After inflation, though, 2022 was still worse: NZ$384 in today's dollars, against NZ$313 on 3 November.

Source: US Energy Information Administration Gulf Coast kerosene-type jet fuel spot price, via FRED; RBNZ exchange rates; Stats NZ consumers price index; US consumer price index (US BLS via FRED).
Units: US and New Zealand dollars per barrel (42 US gallons). Today's dollars use NZ prices at September 2026 and US prices at October 2026.
Data covers: April 1990 to 3 November 2026.
Data last updated: 12 Nov 2026.
