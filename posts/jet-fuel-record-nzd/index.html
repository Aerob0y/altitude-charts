Think filling your car is expensive?

Anyone else keeping an eye on jet fuel prices?

In September, the price reached about NZ$348 a barrel, well above the NZ$326 peak in 2022. Part of the story is the weak NZD. In US dollars, September’s price was below the 2022 peak. Convert it into NZ dollars and it was higher. Allow for inflation, though, and 2022 was still more expensive: about NZ$384 a barrel in September 2026 dollars.

Cheaper fuel helped aviation grow in the second half of the 2010s. I wonder what a sustained period of expensive fuel would do in the other direction?
