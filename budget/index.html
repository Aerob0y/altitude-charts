What these figures mean

Scope: core Crown expenses, including transfers to fund services. This is not total Crown spending or the cost of new Budget initiatives alone. Capital purchases and debt repayments are outside this expense measure. Some expenses, such as depreciation, do not involve a cash payment in the same year.

Time: 2022 means the year ended 30 June 2022. This is a fixed snapshot of Treasury's 29 September 2026 release: 2022–2025 are actuals, 2026 is unaudited actual, and 2027–2031 are forecasts. Later releases can revise these figures.

Changes: amounts are not adjusted for inflation or population growth. Opening a category shows which items account for its change. It does not, on its own, establish why costs changed. Classification changes and temporary programmes can affect comparisons; Treasury's table notes appear with the relevant detail.

Forecast adjustments: future operating allowances and the top-down expense adjustment are separate entries. The latter is negative. Both are included in the total and shares, so the breakdown reconciles.

Download: the reformatted CSV includes high-level spending, tax, balance, revenue and GDP history from 1972, plus this detailed PREFU hierarchy and forecasts. The sources stay separate where their overlapping values differ. Accounting and year-end breaks are labelled.

Next stage: visualising the longer history, inflation and population comparisons, and tax scenarios. This version does not classify spending as forced or optional.

Sources: The Treasury, Pre-election Economic and Fiscal Update 2026, Core Crown Expense Tables; and Fiscal Time Series Historical Fiscal Indicators 1972–2026.
